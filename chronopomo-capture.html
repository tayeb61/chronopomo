Capture syntax
#tag

Anywhere in the line. As many as you like. Casing is kept as typed — matching is case-insensitive, so #Errand and #errand count as the same tag. Wrap in quotes for a tag with spaces or punctuation in it.

Buy milk #errand #home Redo onboarding #"design system"
@category

Anywhere in the line — first one wins if you use more than one.

Renew passport @admin
!priority

Accepted words (case-insensitive):

  • low, l
  • med, medium, m, normal
  • high, hi, h
  • urgent, u, asap, critical, crit
Ship release !urgent
due … / by …

At the end of the line:

  • today, tomorrow, day after tomorrow
  • weekend, next week, next month
  • eow / end of week, eom / end of month
  • in 3 days, in a week, in 2 months
  • a weekday name — friday (nearest upcoming), next friday (the one after)
  • a date — 2026-08-20, 8/20, 8/20/2026, aug 20, 20 aug 2026

Optionally add a time with at — at 3pm, at 15:30, at noon, at midnight.

Call dentist due next tue at 2pm
every …

Recurrence — same date/time words as due. Only one of due/every per line; whichever matches wins.

  • every day / daily, every 3 days
  • every weekday / weekdays
  • every week / weekly, every 2 weeks
  • every month / monthly, every 2 months
  • every monday (a single weekday, repeats weekly)
  • every mon, wed, fri or every 2 weeks on mon/wed/fri — needs 2+ days, separated by comma, slash, &, or and

Any of the above can take at <time> and starting <phrase> (same phrase vocabulary as due).

Water plants every 2 days at 8am Standup every mon, wed, fri starting next monday
remind …

Also remind me …. Two shapes:

  • a one-off — same date/time vocabulary as due
  • a recurring/hourly one — same vocabulary as every, plus every N hours, tried first

Independent of due/every — a line can carry both a due date and its own separate reminder.

Submit report due friday remind tomorrow at 9am Take a break remind every 2 hours
>> sub, sub, sub

Last in the line — everything after >> splits on commas into subtasks.

Plan trip >> book flights, pack bags, print tickets
Multiple tasks

One task per line — each parsed and created separately, in order.

Buy milk #errand Call dentist due friday Water plants every 2 days
/ idea

Start the entry with / to save an idea instead of a task — no due date, no category, no recurrence, just text. Unlike task lines, the whole entry becomes one idea; newlines stay newlines rather than splitting into separate items.

  • #tag — anywhere, same as a task, including the quoted #"multi word" form. Filed under that tag, stripped from the saved text.
  • ==highlight== — wraps text to highlight it (Obsidian-compatible). Kept as-is in the saved text.
  • !pin / !pinned — anywhere, pins it to the top of the ideas list. Stripped from the saved text.
